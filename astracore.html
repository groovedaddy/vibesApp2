Starkeeper Guide

✦ Rarity

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythical. Every captured Star reveals its rarity and full profile.

Win wild battles for Stardust and Lumens. Trainer victories pay more and can unlock quests. Switch when a Star is in danger.
